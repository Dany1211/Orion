Transcript

Rahul (Client):

Good morning everyone. We want to build a modern food delivery platform that connects customers, restaurants, and delivery partners. Initially we'll launch in Pune, but within a year we want to expand to multiple cities.

Sarah (BA):

Who are the primary users of the application?

Rahul:

There will be four major user roles.

Customers who order food.

Restaurant owners who manage menus.

Delivery partners.

Platform administrators.

Dnyanesh:

Can customers register using social login?

Rahul:

Yes.

Google login should be supported.

Mobile OTP login would also be useful later.

For now email and Google are enough.

Sarah:

How should ordering work?

Rahul:

Users should search restaurants, browse menus, customize food items, add items to cart and place an order.

They should be able to apply coupons and pay online.

Cash on Delivery should also be supported.

Omkar:

How will restaurants manage their data?

Rahul:

Restaurant owners should have a dashboard.

They should add or remove menu items.

Update pricing.

Mark dishes unavailable.

View incoming orders.

Accept or reject orders.

Sarah:

What happens after an order is accepted?

Rahul:

The system should automatically assign a nearby delivery partner.

The delivery partner should receive a notification.

Customers should be able to track delivery live.

Dnyanesh:

Should location updates happen in real time?

Rahul:

Yes.

Tracking should refresh every few seconds.

Sarah:

Do you have any security requirements?

Rahul:

Definitely.

Passwords should never be stored in plain text.

Every user should have role-based permissions.

Payment information should remain secure.

Only admins should access analytics.

Omkar:

Any performance expectations?

Rahul:

The application should support at least 10,000 concurrent users during festivals.

Search results should appear almost instantly.

Page loading should not exceed three seconds under normal conditions.

Sarah:

Would you like notifications?

Rahul:

Yes.

Order confirmation.

Restaurant acceptance.

Delivery assignment.

Delivery status updates.

Order delivered.

Promotional notifications later.

Dnyanesh:

Any reports?

Rahul:

Restaurant owners should view daily sales.

Admins need revenue dashboards.

Monthly reports should be downloadable.

Sarah:

Anything else?

Rahul:

Ratings and reviews are important.

Customers should rate restaurants and delivery partners.

Restaurants should respond to reviews.

Omkar:

Any future features?

Rahul:

AI recommendations based on previous orders.

Voice search.

Subscription plans.

Loyalty points.

Dark mode.

Sarah:

Any constraints?

Rahul:

Budget is limited.

We want the MVP in three months.

The system should be cloud-based.

Prefer open-source technologies where possible.

Dnyanesh:

Any integrations?

Rahul:

Google Maps.

Payment Gateway.

SMS service.

Email service.

Push notifications.
